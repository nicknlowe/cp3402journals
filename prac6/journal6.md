## Content Insights

For this sixth week, I learnt the importance of estasblishing good practices to successfully manage a group project, specifically through regular communication and collaborative methods like stand-up meetings and the use of GitHub for version control. This approach ensures that team members have a shared understanding on where the project is and that the project's codebase and files remain accessible and stable throughout the development process.

The use of stand-up meetings provides a dedicated space to catch-up on progress, identify any potential issues and how the team will continue to proceed thanks to the three main questions:
* What did you do yesterday?
* Are there any impediments in your way?
* What will you do today?
In addition to communication measures, technical collaborative tools like GitHub are employed for version control, allowing developers in a team to contribute to the same repository simultaneously without the risk of overwriting each other's work and the ability to rollback to a previous version.

From these practices, I have learnt that clear communication and employing technical tools to assist with team management are essential to reducing team roadblocks and ensuring project continunity. By integrating these habits, a team can transition from individual contributors to a cohesive team unit, ultimately leading to a successful group project and the delivery of a final product.

## Career/Employability/Learning Insights

Upon setting up our group, I focused on establishing the foundational practices to ensure the group had a greater chance of success by creating a Discord server, Trello board and GitHub repo, allowing us to fulfill the regular communication and collaborative method practices of the project. The integration of a service like Discord would allow for persistent communicate with one another, whilst the Trello board serves as a visual roadmap for task delegation and team expectations, mirroring the project management methods often employed by software development firms. Additionally, GitHub serves as a strong platform for version control, allowing us to store and manage code collaboratively while maintaining a clear history and log of contributions. By simulating a professional project development environment, I am directly enhancing my employability as I am demonstrating my ability to navigate both the teamwork and technical aspects of a collaborative project, attributes and knowledge that are in demand by employers within the industry.
